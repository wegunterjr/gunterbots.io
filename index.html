Mission 1: The Robot's Heartbeat

Every robot runs on a loop. This is the whole first program. Press play on the simulator, then open it yourself and make the heart beat faster.

Kids start with blocks in MakeCode.

from microbit import *

while True:
    display.show(Image.HEART)
    sleep(500)
    display.show(Image.HEART_SMALL)
    sleep(500)

The same program in Python. Pro builders can switch to typed code when they're ready. Try it in the micro:bit Python editor.

Open it in MakeCode

No computer handy? Be the robot: one person calls "beat, wait, beat, wait," and the other loops until they hear "stop."

Questions

Before you join

Do we need a robot on day one? ▾

Programming, driving, and sensors run in the free MakeCode simulator — no kit. Physical movement (a rover on the floor, a tape track) needs the Year 1 kit. The kit is about $50–60: micro:bit V2 plus a Cutebot (or Maqueen with board ~$54), bought from the maker, batteries extra. Add-ons like extra servos are optional, never required to finish the week.

What ages is this for? ▾

Kids 8–14 on Rookie, older kids and adults on Pro. Same weekly mission, two difficulty levels. Grown-ups who want to build too are welcome on Pro — same crew, same Thursday.

How much time is this, really? ▾

Plan on 1–2 hours of building during the week. Inventor office hour is Thursday, drop-in, 60–90 minutes. The kid can skip a week and catch up from the library.

Do live sessions need a parent? ▾

Explorers have none: a Monday mission and a build at home. Inventor Thursday is optional drop-in help. The kid comes on with the robot. For kids under 13, a grown-up should be home. Sessions are recorded.

How do you keep kids safe? ▾

The grown-up who pays creates and manages the accounts. There are no private messages between members. Showcase posts are approved before they appear. Office hours are recorded. For kids under 13 we collect only what we need, with parent consent (US COPPA). Chatbot missions can be demoed in office hours so the kid still drives; a grown-up stays nearby because of age limits.

When does it start? ▾

Now. Season 1 is ready, all 12 missions. Join any week and start at Mission 1; each mission unlocks when you finish the one before it, so you go at your own pace. The first 20 families get the founding price. Not ready to pay? Try the free 4-day Robot Starter Challenge first — no kit needed.

Monthly, a season, or summer — what's the difference? ▾

Monthly is the ongoing club, billed each month. Pause any month. A season is 12 weeks of missions plus a catch-up week — three months, paid once. Fall is September–November. Spring is January–March. Inventor includes one Thursday office hour each of those 12 mission weeks. Explorer season is $117 ($39 × 3). Inventor season is $297 ($99 × 3). Same dollars as three monthly bills; pick monthly if you might pause. Summer is Robot Rescue Camp, four weeks in June or July.

What is the founding gift? ▾

For the first 20 families: Explorer $29/mo or Inventor $79/mo (or $87 / $237 a season) for as long as you remain a member. Pause a month and the rate is waiting when you return. Cancel and rejoin later, and public pricing applies. You also get a GN2R pirate patch, a Founding Family badge, and a name on the Founders wall. Honest feedback helps us steer. A public testimonial is optional.

Can we use homeschool or ESA funds? ▾

Inventor at $297 a season is sized for family education accounts: about 7.5% of a Utah Fits All $4,000 award. Arizona ESA, Florida PEP, and Oklahoma's parental-choice tax credit list tutoring and online instruction as eligible categories. We are not automatically an approved vendor. Ask your program before you spend, and keep the kit (~$50–60) as a separate materials line.
