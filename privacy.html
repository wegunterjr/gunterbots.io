Privacy

Privacy and kid safety

Robot Logic Lab is a Gunterbots school, run by Gunterbots LLC. It is built for families. Parents own the relationship.

What we collect on this waitlist

Your name, email, who is building (age band, not a child's name), which seat you want, and anything you type in the optional note. We use that to send the starter challenge and to tell you when founding spots open. We do not sell this list.

When the club is running

  • Parents create and manage every account.
  • We do not collect emails from kids under 13. US COPPA: we collect only what we need, with parent consent.
  • No private messages between members.
  • Showcase posts are approved before anyone else sees them. Robot-only videos are welcome — no faces needed.
  • Live office hours are recorded.
  • Kids never need a chatbot account. Chatbot missions happen with a grown-up.

Robot Rules for AI

  • Never share a name, address, school, photos, or passwords with an AI.
  • AI can be confidently wrong. Test it before you trust it.
  • Use chatbots with a grown-up. Many have minimum ages.
  • AI is a tool, not a friend.
  • Say when AI helped. You are the engineer.
